## What you already know
You have seen a tube light and a fan work because they are connected to electricity by wires. In the same way, the parts of a computer are joined to each other by cables.

## Lesson
Think about a tube light and a fan at home. They work because wires connect them. A computer works in a similar way. Its parts are joined together by cables.

Because all the parts are connected with one another, we call a computer a **system**.

The cables that join the parts are called **connecting cables**. They come in different sizes. Each cable has its own use. Here are some common cables and what they do.

**VGA cable:** It connects the computer monitor to the CPU.

**HDMI cable:** It sends high quality sound and pictures. It connects a monitor or a projector to the computer.

**USB cable:** Many devices use a USB cable or cord to connect to the computer. These include a printer, a pendrive, a scanner, a mouse, a keyboard, a web camera and a mobile phone.

**Data cable:** It carries data. It connects a tablet or a mobile phone to the CPU so that data can move between them.

**Audio jack:** It connects a speaker to the computer.

**Power cord:** It connects an appliance to the main electricity supply. It does this for a short time when needed.

**Mic cable:** It connects a microphone to the CPU.

**Ethernet cable:** It helps the computer connect to the internet.

Remember: each cable has one job. Choose the right cable for the right part.

## Key words
- **System**: a group of parts that are connected and work together.
- **Cable**: a wire used to connect one thing to another.
- **Connecting cable**: a cable that joins parts of a computer.
- **CPU**: the part of the computer that the other parts are connected to in this lesson.
- **VGA**: a type of cable that connects the monitor to the CPU.
- **HDMI**: a type of cable that sends high quality sound and pictures to a monitor or projector.
- **USB**: a type of cable used to connect many devices, such as a printer or a mouse.
- **Data cable**: a cable that carries data between a device and the CPU.
- **Audio jack**: a connector used to join a speaker to the computer.
- **Ethernet**: a type of cable that helps the computer connect to the internet.

## Practice questions
1. Which cable connects the computer monitor to the CPU?
2. Why do we call a computer a system?
3. Name two devices that are connected to the computer with a USB cable.
4. What does an HDMI cable do?
5. Ravi wants to show his computer screen on a big projector in class. Which cable should he use?

## Answers
1. The VGA cable connects the computer monitor to the CPU.
2. We call a computer a system because its parts are connected with one another.
3. A printer and a mouse are connected with a USB cable. (Any two of: printer, pendrive, scanner, mouse, keyboard, web camera, mobile phone.)
4. An HDMI cable sends high quality audio and video. It connects a monitor or projector to the computer.
5. Ravi should use an HDMI cable, because it connects a projector to the computer.
