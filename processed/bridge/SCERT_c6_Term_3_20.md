## What you already know
You already know that we use water for drinking, cooking, bathing, and watering plants, and that seawater is too salty for farms and factories. This lesson goes further: how water works inside our body, how much water a family uses, and why swamps matter.

## Lesson
Water is very important to us. Let us look at four places where we need it.

**Water in our body**
Our body is made of tiny parts called cells. Water is found in all our cells, organs, and tissues. A tissue is a group of cells that work together. Water helps to keep our body temperature steady. Body temperature is how hot or cold our body is.

Water also helps us with digestion. Digestion is the way the body breaks down food so it can use it. Water helps remove toxins from the body. Toxins are harmful waste substances.

On average, the human body needs 2 to 3 litres of water every day to work properly.

**Water at home**
We drink water. We also use it for cooking, bathing, washing clothes, washing utensils, cleaning our houses and common places, and watering plants. Domestic means "related to the home." So these are domestic uses of water.

Think about one day in your family. Water is used for brushing teeth, bathing, washing clothes, flushing toilets, cooking, washing utensils, and cleaning the floor. Adding up all this water gives the total water a family uses in one day. Your class can do Activity 6 to estimate this.

**Water in farms and factories**
Water helps farm crops and farm animals grow healthy. Water is also used to make many products.

Industry depends on water at every level of production. Industry uses water in three ways:
- as a material, which means it becomes part of the product,
- as a solvent, which is a liquid that can dissolve other substances,
- for generating electricity.

**Swamps and wetlands**
A wetland is a wet area of land. A swamp is a wetland that has forest. Swamps are found along large rivers or on the shores of large lakes. The water in a swamp may be freshwater, brackish water, or seawater. Brackish water is water that is neither fully fresh nor fully salty.

Swamps are important because they provide fresh water and oxygen to all living things. Some swamps in Tamil Nadu are the Pichavaram mangroves in Chidambaram, the Muthupet mangrove wetland, the Pallikkaranai wetland in Chennai, and Chembarambakkam in Kancheepuram.

## Key words
- **digestion**: breaking down food so the body can use it
- **toxins**: harmful waste substances in the body
- **domestic**: related to the home
- **agriculture**: farming
- **industry**: factories and businesses that make goods
- **solvent**: a liquid that can dissolve other substances
- **wetland**: a wet area of land
- **swamp**: a wetland that has forest
- **brackish water**: water that is neither fully fresh nor fully salty

## Practice questions
1. On average, how many litres of water does the human body need each day?
2. Name three uses of water at home.
3. How does water help our body?
4. Name the three ways industry uses water.
5. Think about your home. Name two ways your family uses water in one day.

## Answers
1. On average, the human body needs 2 to 3 litres of water each day.
2. Water is used for cooking, bathing, washing clothes, washing utensils, cleaning houses, and watering plants. (Any three.)
3. Water helps keep body temperature steady, helps with digestion, and helps remove toxins from the body.
4. Industry uses water as a material, as a solvent, and for generating electricity.
5. Answers will vary. For example, we use water for bathing and cooking.
