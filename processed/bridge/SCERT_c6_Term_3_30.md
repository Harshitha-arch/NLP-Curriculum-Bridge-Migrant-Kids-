## What you already know

Your family may use an oily liquid to clean the floor or the bathroom. This lesson tells you the name of a chemical that is used in such cleaning materials. The passage asks you to think about this oily material.

## Lesson

Have you seen the oily material that your family uses to clean the house? It is called **phenol**. Phenol is a chemical.

Phenol is also called **carbolic acid**. It is a type of **organic compound**, which is a group of chemicals. Phenol is a **weak acid**. This means it is an acid that is not very strong.

Scientists write each chemical with a short code. This code is called the **molecular formula**. The molecular formula of phenol is C6H5OH.

Phenol is a **volatile** white **crystalline** powder. Volatile means it changes easily into a gas or vapour. Crystalline means it is made of small, regular pieces called crystals.

Phenol is a necessary **ingredient** for making many phenol products. An ingredient is one of the things you mix together to make something.

The lesson also talks about two other chemicals: **gypsum** and **Epsom salt**.

Gypsum has the molecular formula CaSO4·2H2O. Gypsum is used as a **fertilizer**. A fertilizer is something we add to soil to help plants grow. Gypsum is also used in making **cement**. Cement is used in building. It is also used in making **Plaster of Paris**.

Epsom salt is magnesium sulphate heptahydrate. Its molecular formula is MgSO4·7H2O. Epsom salt has many uses. It eases stress and helps the body relax. It helps muscles and nerves work properly. It is used as a medicine for skin problems. It also improves plant growth in farming.

Remember: phenol is the oily cleaning material. Gypsum and Epsom salt are different chemicals with different uses.

## Key words

- **Phenol**: a chemical found in the oily material used to clean houses
- **Carbolic acid**: another name for phenol
- **Weak acid**: an acid that is not very strong
- **Molecular formula**: a short code that shows what a chemical is made of
- **Volatile**: changes easily into a gas or vapour
- **Crystalline**: made of small, regular crystals
- **Ingredient**: one of the things mixed together to make something
- **Gypsum**: a chemical used as fertilizer and in making cement
- **Fertilizer**: something added to soil to help plants grow
- **Epsom salt**: magnesium sulphate heptahydrate, used for many purposes including medicine

## Practice questions

1. What is the name of the oily material used to clean your house?
2. What does the word "volatile" mean?
3. Name two uses of gypsum.
4. Why is phenol called a necessary ingredient?
5. Your family cleans the floor with an oily liquid. What is the name of the chemical in that liquid, according to this lesson?

## Answers

1. The oily material used to clean the house is phenol.
2. Volatile means it changes easily into a gas or vapour.
3. Gypsum is used as a fertilizer and in the process of making cement.
4. Phenol is needed to make many phenol products, so it is a necessary ingredient.
5. According to the lesson, the chemical in the oily cleaning material is phenol.
