## What you already know
In your NCERT book, you learned that food contains nutrients such as carbohydrates, fats and proteins. You also learned that carbohydrates and fats give us energy. Now you will learn what happens when our food does not have the right mix of nutrients.

## Lesson
Food helps your body grow and work. A **balanced diet** is food that has a sufficient amount of many different nutrients. **Nutrients** are the parts of food that the body needs. A balanced diet helps keep you healthy.

A balanced diet is important for four reasons:
- It increases your capacity to work.
- It gives good physical and mental health.
- It increases your capacity to resist diseases.
- It helps your body grow properly.

Now think about what happens when the diet is not balanced. Some children in the passage do not look normal. Their health is poor because of **malnutrition**. Malnutrition happens when the body does not get all the nutrients it needs, in the right amounts, from food. Malnutrition can lead to **deficiency diseases**. These are diseases caused by a lack of nutrients in the diet.

Here are some examples:
- A lack of iron causes **anaemia**.
- A lack of calcium causes **rickets**.
- A lack of iodine causes **goitre** in adults and **cretinism** in children.
- A lack of protein can cause **kwashiorkor**. A child with kwashiorkor may have stunted growth and swelling of the face, limbs and belly, and may have diarrhoea.
- Another protein-related disease is **marasmus**. A child with marasmus looks very thin and grows slowly.

Your teacher may ask you to make a diet chart for a 12-year-old boy or girl. Use foods that are not expensive and are commonly available in your area.

## Key words
- **balanced diet**: food that has enough of many different nutrients
- **nutrients**: parts of food that the body needs
- **malnutrition**: poor health that happens when the body does not get the right nutrients in the right amounts
- **deficiency diseases**: diseases caused by a lack of nutrients in the diet
- **anaemia**: a deficiency disease caused by a lack of iron
- **rickets**: a deficiency disease caused by a lack of calcium
- **goitre**: a deficiency disease in adults caused by a lack of iodine
- **kwashiorkor**: a protein deficiency disease with stunted growth and swelling of the face, limbs and belly
- **marasmus**: a protein deficiency disease in which the child looks skinny and grows slowly
- **obese**: having too much body fat, which can cause health problems

## Practice questions
1. What is a balanced diet?
2. Name two reasons why a balanced diet is important.
3. What is malnutrition?
4. Which mineral's lack causes anaemia?
5. The passage says a diet chart should use food items that are not expensive and are commonly available in your area. Why is this useful?

## Answers
1. A balanced diet is food that has a sufficient amount of many different nutrients to keep us healthy.
2. A balanced diet increases our capacity to work and helps our body grow properly. It also gives good physical and mental health and helps us resist diseases.
3. Malnutrition happens when the body does not get all the nutrients it needs, in the right amounts, from food.
4. Iron. A lack of iron causes anaemia.
5. Such foods are easy to buy, so a family can actually follow the diet chart.

## Note for the teacher
This lesson is based on the SCERT "Health and Nutrients" passage, with NCERT Mindful Eating passages used only for the "What you already know" section. The SCERT passage spells the disease "Kwashiorkar" and gives only brief symptoms for goitre, cretinism and rickets, so the lesson gives only those details and uses the standard spelling "kwashiorkor."
