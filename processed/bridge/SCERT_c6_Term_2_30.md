## What you already know

You have learnt from your NCERT book that the Sun, water and forests are natural resources, and that people cut down trees in forests. Today you will learn how changes around us are sorted into those that nature makes and those that people make.

## Lesson

Many things around us keep changing. Some changes happen on their own. Some changes happen only because people make them.

**Natural changes**

A **natural change** is a change that takes place in nature on its own. People cannot control it. Three examples are:

- **Rotation of the earth**: The earth turns around itself. This is called **rotation**.
- **Changing phases of the Moon**: The Moon looks different at different times. Each shape it shows is called a **phase**.
- **Rain**: Water falls from the sky.

**Human-made changes**

A **human-made change** is also called an **artificial change**. People bring it about. It will not happen on its own. Four examples are:

- **Cooking**: We cook food, and the food changes. Rain does not cook food. A person must cook it.
- **Deforestation**: This means cutting down forests. You learnt in your NCERT book that many trees are cut by people, so the forest cover is decreasing.
- **Cultivating crops**: Farmers prepare land and grow crops such as rice on it.
- **Construction of buildings**: People build houses, schools and other buildings.

**Remember this**

Ask yourself one question: "Did a person cause this change?"

- If no, it is a natural change. Rain falls on its own.
- If yes, it is a human-made change. Cooking happens only when a person cooks.

## Key words

- **Natural change**: a change that happens in nature on its own, without people controlling it.
- **Human-made change**: a change that people bring about. It does not happen on its own.
- **Artificial change**: another name for a human-made change.
- **Rotation**: turning around itself. The earth does this.
- **Phase**: one of the different shapes the Moon shows at different times.
- **Deforestation**: cutting down forests.
- **Cultivating**: growing crops by farming the land.
- **Construction**: building things such as houses and buildings.

## Practice questions

1. Name two natural changes from the lesson.
2. What is the difference between a natural change and a human-made change?
3. Why does cooking food count as a human-made change?
4. Give one example of deforestation and explain why it is a human-made change.
5. Name one change you saw around you today. Say whether it was natural or human-made, and give a reason.

## Answers

1. Two natural changes are rain and the rotation of the earth.
2. A natural change happens in nature on its own and people cannot control it. A human-made change is brought about by people and will not happen on its own.
3. Cooking is a human-made change because a person must cook the food. The food does not change on its own.
4. Cutting down trees in a forest is deforestation. It is human-made because people do the cutting.
5. The answer depends on what the child saw. For example, if the child saw rain, it is natural because nobody makes it fall. If the child saw a person cooking, it is human-made because a person did it.
