## What you already know

You may have used cello tape to fix a torn page, or glue to stick paper together. The sticky material on the tape or glue is an adhesive.

## Lesson

Think about a torn page in your book. How can we fix it? We can use cello tape. One side of the tape has a sticky material on it. This sticky material is called an **adhesive**.

An adhesive is a substance that sticks two things together. It is put on one surface, or on both surfaces, of two separate items. It joins them and stops them from coming apart. Adhesives are also called glue, mucilage, or paste.

Look at how a bicycle puncture is repaired. The shopkeeper first makes sure the punctured area is clean, dry, and free of dust. He rubs the area around the hole with a metal scraper so it becomes rough. Then he takes a patch of tyre tube and puts a suitable adhesive on the rough area and on the patch. He presses firmly and waits for it to dry completely.

Why does he press firmly? Pressing helps the adhesive hold both surfaces together and makes the join strong.

There are two kinds of adhesives:

- **Natural adhesives** are made from starch.
- **Artificial adhesives** are made from chemicals.

The adhesive used in a puncture shop is an artificial adhesive.

Adhesives come in different forms. They can be a paste, a liquid, a film, pellets, or a tape. The cello tape on your torn page is one form.

## Key words

- **Adhesive**: a sticky substance that joins two things together
- **Mucilage**: another name for a sticky glue-like material
- **Paste**: a thick, soft, sticky substance
- **Starch**: a natural material from plants, used to make some natural adhesives
- **Artificial**: made by people, not found in nature
- **Puncture**: a small hole in a tyre or tube
- **Patch**: a small piece used to cover a hole or tear
- **Pressure**: a firm push on something

## Practice questions

1. What is an adhesive?
2. What does the shopkeeper do to the punctured area before he puts on the adhesive?
3. Why does the shopkeeper press the patch firmly?
4. Name the two kinds of adhesives and say what each is made from.
5. You want to fix a torn page in your notebook. What could you use, and what would you do to make it stick?

## Answers

1. An adhesive is a sticky substance that joins two or more things together.
2. He makes sure the area is clean, dry, and free of dust, and he roughens it with a metal scraper.
3. Pressing firmly increases the adhesive's hold on both surfaces and makes the join strong.
4. Natural adhesives are made from starch, and artificial adhesives are made from chemicals.
5. You could use cello tape or glue. Stick the tape or glue on the torn edges, press them together firmly, and let it dry if needed.
