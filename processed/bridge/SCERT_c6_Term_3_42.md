## What you already know
In your NCERT lesson on water, you saw plastic bags and wrappers floating on rivers and lakes, because people throw trash into them. This kind of harm to our surroundings has a name: pollution.

## Lesson
Pollution is damage to the environment. The environment is everything around us, such as our land, air and water. Pollution is mostly caused by human activities, which means things people do.

Pollution happens when the environment gets contaminated. Contaminated means made dirty or impure by something harmful. The things that contaminate the environment are wastes, chemicals and harmful substances. For example, wastes that are thrown into water make it dirty.

Any substance that causes pollution is called a pollutant. A substance is any material. So a waste, a chemical or a harmful substance that causes pollution is a pollutant.

Pollution is an unwanted change in our land, air and water. These have physical, chemical and biological characteristics. Physical means to do with how a thing looks and feels. Chemical means to do with what a thing is made of. Biological means to do with living things. Pollution changes these characteristics in a way that nobody wants.

There are four major kinds of pollution:

1. Air pollution
2. Water pollution
3. Land (soil) pollution
4. Noise pollution

Remember these four kinds. Each one is pollution of a different part of our surroundings, or pollution by noise.

## Key words
- **Pollution**: damage to the environment, mostly caused by human activities
- **Environment**: everything around us, such as land, air and water
- **Contaminated**: made dirty or impure by something harmful
- **Pollutant**: any substance that causes pollution
- **Substance**: any material
- **Characteristics**: the features that make a thing what it is
- **Physical**: to do with how a thing looks and feels
- **Chemical**: to do with what a thing is made of
- **Biological**: to do with living things

## Practice questions
1. What is pollution?
2. What is a pollutant?
3. Name the four major kinds of pollution.
4. Why is a waste that is thrown into a river a pollutant?
5. Plastic wrappers are floating in a pond near your home. Is this pollution? Why?

## Answers
1. Pollution is damage to the environment, mostly caused by human activities.
2. A pollutant is any substance that causes pollution.
3. The four major kinds of pollution are air pollution, water pollution, land (soil) pollution and noise pollution.
4. It is a pollutant because it is a waste that contaminates the water and causes pollution.
5. Yes, it is pollution, because the wrappers are wastes that make the pond dirty, which is damage to the environment.

## Note for the teacher
This lesson is based on the SCERT passage on pollution, with the water examples from the NCERT water lesson used only in "What you already know" and the answer to question 5. The SCERT passage names the four kinds of pollution but does not describe each one or give examples, so the lesson does not explain them further.
