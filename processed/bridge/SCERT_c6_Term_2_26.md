## What you already know

You have seen your hair and nails grow over many weeks, and you have seen a balloon pop in a moment. Both are changes, but one is slow and one is fast.

## Lesson

Look around you. Things are always changing. Some changes are slow, and some are fast.

A **change** is when something becomes different. Hair grows longer. A balloon pops. Paper burns.

**Slow changes** take place over a long time. This can be hours, days, months or years. Here are some examples:

- Your nails and hair grow. You do not see them grow in one minute, but after some time they are longer.
- The seasons change. Summer turns into winter over many months.
- A seed begins to grow into a plant. This is called **germination**. It takes time.

**Fast changes** take place in a short time. This can be seconds or minutes. Here are some examples:

- A balloon bursts. You hear it in a second.
- A glass breaks. It happens in a moment.
- Fire crackers burst. They make their sound and light in a few seconds.
- Paper burns. The paper changes quickly when it catches fire.

So, the main difference is time. If a change takes seconds or minutes, it is fast. If a change takes hours, days, months or years, it is slow.

Think of your own day. Your shadow moves slowly as the sun moves. When you drop a cup, it breaks fast. Both are changes around us.

## Key words

- **change**: when something becomes different
- **slow change**: a change that takes place over hours, days, months or years
- **fast change**: a change that takes place in seconds or minutes
- **germination**: when a seed begins to grow into a plant
- **season**: one of the long parts of the year, such as summer or winter

## Practice questions

1. What is a slow change?
2. What is a fast change?
3. Name one slow change from the lesson.
4. Name one fast change from the lesson.
5. Your friend says a plant growing from a seed is a fast change. Is your friend right? Explain why.

## Answers

1. A slow change is a change that takes place over a long period of time, such as hours, days, months or years.
2. A fast change is a change that takes place within a short period of time, such as seconds or minutes.
3. Growth of nails and hair is a slow change (also: change of seasons, or germination of seed).
4. Bursting of a balloon is a fast change (also: breaking of glass, bursting of fire crackers, or burning of paper).
5. Your friend is not right. A seed growing into a plant takes a long time, so it is a slow change.

## Note for the teacher

This lesson is based only on the SCERT passage "Slow and Fast changes", with its examples. The passage gives no explanation of why some changes are fast and others slow, so the lesson does not add one; the child's own daily experience is used instead.
