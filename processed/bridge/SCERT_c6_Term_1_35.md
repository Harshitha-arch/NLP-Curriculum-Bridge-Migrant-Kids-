## What you already know
You already wash your hands before eating and brush your teeth in the morning. Keeping your body and home clean is part of staying healthy, and this lesson explains why.

## Lesson
Health means more than not being ill. The World Health Organization (WHO) says health is a state of complete physical, mental, and social well-being. Physical means the body. Mental means the mind. Social means how well we get along with other people. Health is not merely the absence of disease. A disease is an illness.

Health is also dynamic. Dynamic means always changing. Your body is always adjusting and adapting to stresses and to changes in the environment. The environment is everything around us. Your body keeps an inner balance, called equilibrium. This balance inside the body is called homeostasis.

Healthcare exists to help people stay healthy.

Hygiene is the practice of keeping yourself and your surroundings clean. Good hygiene helps prevent illness and the spread of diseases. Brushing your teeth regularly is an important part of good oral hygiene. Oral means of the mouth.

Look at an everyday example. Deepa saw a list of food for her family, with raw rice 25 kg, wheat 5 kg, cooking oil 2 litres, and ghee 500 grams. She asked her parents why they eat more rice and wheat than oil and ghee. Discuss this list with your teacher.

## Key words
- **health**: a state of complete physical, mental, and social well-being
- **hygiene**: keeping yourself and your surroundings clean to prevent illness
- **disease**: an illness
- **healthcare**: help given to people to stay healthy
- **dynamic**: always changing
- **environment**: everything around us
- **equilibrium**: a balance
- **homeostasis**: the body's inner balance
- **oral hygiene**: keeping the mouth clean, for example by brushing teeth

## Practice questions
1. What does the World Health Organization (WHO) say health is?
2. What is hygiene?
3. Why does your body keep adjusting itself?
4. Look at Deepa's list. Which item is listed in the largest amount?
5. Why is brushing your teeth regularly a good habit?

## Answers
1. WHO says health is a state of complete physical, mental, and social well-being, not merely the absence of disease.
2. Hygiene is the practice of keeping yourself and your surroundings clean, in order to prevent illness or the spread of diseases.
3. Your body adjusts to stresses and changes in the environment so that it can keep its inner balance, called homeostasis.
4. Raw rice is listed in the largest amount, 25 kg.
5. Brushing your teeth is an important part of good oral hygiene, and it helps keep your mouth clean.

## Note for the teacher
This lesson is based only on the SCERT passage on "Health and Hygiene." The passage asks why Deepa's family eats more rice and wheat than oil and ghee but does not answer this, so the lesson leaves it for class discussion. The passage also calls hygiene a "science" in one sentence and a "practice" in another, and the lesson uses the practice meaning.
