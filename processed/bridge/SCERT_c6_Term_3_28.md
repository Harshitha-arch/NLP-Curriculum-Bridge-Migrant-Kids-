## What you already know
Your NCERT book taught you that all things are made of materials, and that clay can be baked to make strong pots. Soil also forms when rocks break apart over a very long time.

## Lesson
Long ago, people built houses with a mixture of lime, sand and wood. Today, people widely use cement to build houses, dams and bridges.

Cement is a powder that hardens when mixed with water. It is made from natural minerals. A mineral is a natural substance found in the earth. The minerals used to make cement are lime, clay and gypsum. These minerals are crushed through a process called milling. Milling means grinding the minerals into a fine powder.

Cement is also used to make a very strong material called reinforced cement concrete. "Reinforced" means made stronger. To make it, iron mesh is mixed with cement. Iron mesh is a net-like grid of iron wires. Reinforced cement concrete is a composite material, which means it is made by joining two different materials together. Cement and iron mesh together make it very strong and firm.

Because it is so strong, reinforced cement concrete is used in many places:
- dams and bridges
- pillars
- centering work in houses (the temporary frame that holds wet concrete until it hardens)
- huge water tanks, water pipes and drains

So, in the past, people used lime, sand and wood. Now, people use cement and reinforced cement concrete to build strong structures.

## Key words
- **Cement**: a powder that is mixed with water and used for building, which becomes hard as it dries.
- **Mineral**: a natural substance found in the earth.
- **Lime**: a white mineral used to make cement.
- **Clay**: a soft earth material that is used to make pots and bricks.
- **Gypsum**: a mineral that is one of the materials used to make cement.
- **Milling**: a process of grinding things into a powder.
- **Composite material**: a material made by joining two different materials together.
- **Iron mesh**: a net-like grid of iron wires.
- **Reinforced cement concrete**: a strong building material made by mixing iron mesh with cement.
- **Pillar**: a tall, upright support that holds up part of a building.

## Practice questions
1. What materials were used to build houses in ancient times?
2. Name the three minerals used to make cement.
3. How is cement made from natural minerals?
4. What is reinforced cement concrete made of?
5. Why do you think reinforced cement concrete is used to build dams and bridges?

## Answers
1. In ancient times, houses were built using a mixture of lime, sand and wood.
2. The three minerals used to make cement are lime, clay and gypsum.
3. Cement is made by crushing naturally occurring minerals through a milling process.
4. Reinforced cement concrete is made by mixing iron mesh with cement.
5. It is very strong and firm, which makes it suitable for large structures like dams and bridges.
