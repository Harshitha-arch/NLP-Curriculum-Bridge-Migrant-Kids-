## What you already know

You have read that food in different parts of India is based on the crops grown there. Spices are another part of our food, and they are often added to make a dish taste and smell good.

## Lesson

Today we learn about **spices**. A spice is a part of a plant that we use to make food tasty.

Spices are **aromatic** parts of plants. Aromatic means they have a strong, pleasant smell. Spices grow on **tropical** plants. Tropical plants grow in warm parts of the world.

Spices come from different parts of a plant. They can come from the **bark** (the outer covering of a stem or tree), the **roots** (the parts that grow under the soil), the **leaves**, the **flowers**, or the **stems**.

People use spices mainly for three things:

- **Flavouring**: to give food a taste.
- **Colouring**: to give food a colour.
- **Preserving**: to keep food from going bad.

Many Indian dishes use spices. Here are some spices used in India:

- Cardamom
- Black pepper
- Curry leaves
- Fenugreek
- Fennel
- Ajwain
- Bay leaves
- Cumin
- Coriander seeds
- Turmeric
- Cloves
- Ginger
- Nutmeg
- Cinnamon

Look in your kitchen at home. You may find some of these spices in a box or jar. Next time you smell a spice, think about the plant it came from.

Remember: a spice is a part of a plant used to flavour, colour or preserve food.

## Key words

- **Spice**: a part of a plant used to give food flavour, colour or to keep it from going bad.
- **Aromatic**: having a strong, pleasant smell.
- **Tropical**: growing in warm parts of the world.
- **Flavouring**: adding taste to food.
- **Colouring**: adding colour to food.
- **Preserving**: keeping food from going bad.
- **Bark**: the outer covering of a stem or tree.
- **Roots**: the parts of a plant that grow under the soil.
- **Cardamom**: a spice used in Indian dishes.
- **Cinnamon**: a spice used in Indian dishes.

## Practice questions

1. From which parts of a plant can spices come? Name any two.
2. Name the three main uses of spices.
3. What does "aromatic" mean?
4. Name any two spices used in Indian dishes.
5. There is a jar of cloves in your kitchen. What is cloves used for, and which part of its plant family group does it belong to?

Note: Question 5 should be rewritten by the teacher, because the lesson does not say which part of the plant cloves come from.

## Answers

1. Spices can come from the bark, roots, leaves, flowers or stems of plants.
2. Spices are mainly used for flavouring, colouring and preserving food.
3. Aromatic means having a strong, pleasant smell.
4. Any two of: cardamom, black pepper, curry leaves, fenugreek, fennel, ajwain, bay leaves, cumin, coriander seeds, turmeric, cloves, ginger, nutmeg, cinnamon.
5. Cloves are used to flavour food. (The lesson does not say which plant part cloves come from.)
