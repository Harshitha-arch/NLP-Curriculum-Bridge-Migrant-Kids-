## What you already know
You know that a tiny ant and a big tree are both living things. A wall is built from many small bricks, and the whole wall is made of those pieces.

## Lesson
Every living thing is made of cells. A cell is a very small unit of life. Some living things have only one cell. Others have many cells.

**Unicellular** means "single cell." Bacteria, Amoeba, Chlamydomonas and Yeast are unicellular. Each of them is just one cell.

**Multicellular** means "many cells." Spirogyra, mango trees and human beings are multicellular. A multicellular living thing is made of a few hundred to millions of cells.

The human body has about 3.7 × 10¹³ cells. That is a very big number.

Cells can be very different in size. The largest cell is the egg of an ostrich. It is 170 millimetres wide. We can see it with the **naked eye**, which means seeing without a microscope. In the human body, nerve cells are believed to be the longest cells.

A big animal does not always have bigger cells. An elephant's cells are not necessarily much bigger than a mouse's cells. The size of a cell has no link to the size of the animal.

**A model using a hen's egg:** Break a hen's egg onto a plate and look at it. The thin skin on the inside of the shell is like the **cell membrane**, the outer covering of a cell. The clear, jelly-like white is like the **cytoplasm**, the jelly-like material inside the cell. The thicker yellow part is like the **nucleus**, the control centre of the cell. This is only a model to help you picture a cell.

**Two types of cells:**
- A **prokaryotic cell** has no true nucleus and no nuclear membrane.
- A **eukaryotic cell** has a true nucleus. The nucleus has a **nuclear membrane**, a covering around it. Eukaryotic cells are bigger than prokaryotic cells. Their **organelles**, which are small parts inside the cell, are surrounded by a membrane.

Plants, animals, most fungi and most algae have eukaryotic cells.

## Key words
- **Unicellular**: made of one cell.
- **Multicellular**: made of many cells.
- **Cell membrane**: the outer covering of a cell.
- **Cytoplasm**: the jelly-like material inside a cell.
- **Nucleus**: the part of the cell that controls it.
- **Nuclear membrane**: the covering around the nucleus.
- **Prokaryotic cell**: a cell with no true nucleus.
- **Eukaryotic cell**: a cell with a true nucleus.
- **Organelle**: a small part inside a cell.
- **Naked eye**: seeing without a microscope.

## Practice questions
1. What is a unicellular living thing? Name one from the lesson.
2. Name two multicellular living things from the lesson.
3. Why is cell size not linked to animal size? Use the elephant and the mouse in your answer.
4. What is the difference between a prokaryotic cell and a eukaryotic cell?
5. Human beings are animals. What type of cell do human beings have, and why?

## Answers
1. A unicellular living thing is made of one cell. Bacteria, Amoeba, Chlamydomonas and Yeast are examples.
2. Spirogyra, mango and human beings are multicellular. Any two of these is correct.
3. An elephant's cells are not necessarily much bigger than a mouse's cells, so the size of the animal does not depend on the size of its cells.
4. A prokaryotic cell has no true nucleus and no nuclear membrane. A eukaryotic cell has a true nucleus with a nuclear membrane.
5. Human beings have eukaryotic cells, because animals have eukaryotic cells and the cell has a true nucleus.
