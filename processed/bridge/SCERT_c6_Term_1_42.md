## What you already know
You have probably seen a shop bill printed after the shopkeeper uses a machine to add up your items. You may also use a mobile phone to make calls, take photos, or play games. Today you will learn that these are all forms of computers.

## Lesson
Many people think a computer is only a big box with a TV screen. But computers come in different shapes. A laptop and a tablet are types of computers. A smart phone can also do many of the same jobs as a computer. Computers may differ in speed, but they work in the same way.

**What is a computer?**
A computer is an electronic device. An electronic device is a machine that works with electricity. A computer processes data and information for us, according to our needs. It can save data and change it into information.

**Data and information**
Data is the raw material that a computer works on. It is often in the form of numbers, letters, or pictures. It cannot be used directly by us until it is processed. Information is processed data. It is data that we can use.

**Where are computers used?**
Computers are used in textile shops for billing. They are also used in railway stations, banks, ATM machines, post offices, and schools.

**A short history**
Long ago, people used a tool called the abacus for calculations. Computers did not come directly from the abacus. Computers are grouped into five generations:
- First generation: vacuum tubes
- Second generation: transistors
- Third generation: integrated circuits
- Fourth generation: microprocessors
- Fifth generation: artificial intelligence

Today's computers belong to the fifth generation.

**Two people in computer history**
In the early 19th century, Charles Babbage was a professor of mathematics. He designed a computer. He is known as the father of computer. The basic structure he designed is used in all computers today. Augusta Ada Lovelace is known as the first programmer. She developed essential commands for mathematical operations.

**Hardware and software**
Hardware is the parts of the computer that help the software to work. Software is the set of commands or programs used in a computer. Software is of two types:
- Operating software: software used to operate the computer.
- Application software: software for tasks such as painting and playing games.

**Input and output devices**
We send things to a computer through input devices, such as the keyboard and mouse. The data or information is shown or printed through output devices, such as the printer and monitor.

**CPU**
CPU stands for central processing unit. You will learn more about it in higher classes.

## Key words
- **Computer**: an electronic device that processes data and information according to our needs.
- **Data**: information that has to be processed before we can use it.
- **Information**: processed data that we can use.
- **Software**: the commands or programs used in a computer.
- **Hardware**: the parts of a computer that help the software to work.
- **Input device**: a device used to send data to a computer, such as a keyboard or mouse.
- **Output device**: a device that shows or prints the data or information, such as a printer or monitor.
- **Operating software**: software that is used to operate the computer.
- **Application software**: software for tasks such as painting and playing games.
- **CPU**: central processing unit, the main processing part of a computer.

## Practice questions
1. Who is known as the father of computer?
2. Name two input devices and two output devices.
3. What is the difference between data and information?
4. What are the two types of software?
5. Name one thing you can do on a smart phone that a computer can also do.

## Answers
1. Charles Babbage is known as the father of computer.
2. Input devices include the keyboard and mouse. Output devices include the printer and monitor.
3. Data is information that has to be processed. Information is processed data.
4. The two types of software are operating software and application software.
5. You can draw, paint, play games, or learn on a smart phone, just as on a computer.
