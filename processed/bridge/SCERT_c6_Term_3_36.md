## What you already know

You learnt in NCERT that animals and plants are different in different places. For example, fish have fins for swimming, and cactus plants in the desert have thick stems. This lesson looks at the whole space around living things, which we call their environment.

## Lesson

Look around you. Your classroom, the trees outside, the sun, the air you breathe and the water you drink are all parts of your **environment**.

An environment is the surroundings or space in which a person, an animal or a plant lives. It is everything that surrounds us.

An environment has two kinds of things in it.

**Non-living things** are called **abiotic** factors. They are not alive. Examples are sunlight, air, water and minerals in soil. Sunlight, air and water are all around you, even inside your classroom.

**Living things** are called **biotic** factors. They are alive. Examples are plants, animals and bacteria. Bacteria are very small living things. You cannot see them without a microscope.

Living things do not live alone. **Organisms**, which are living things, constantly **interact** with one another. To interact means to act on each other or affect each other. A plant and an animal living near each other interact in many ways.

Organisms also **adapt** to the conditions of their environment. To adapt means to change or fit yourself to suit the place where you live. You saw this in NCERT, where fish have fins for water and cactus stores water in its stem. Both are ways of adapting.

So, an environment is made of living and non-living things, and all of them are connected.

## Key words

- **environment**: the surroundings or space in which a living thing lives
- **abiotic**: non-living things in the environment, such as sunlight, air, water and minerals in soil
- **biotic**: living things in the environment, such as plants, animals and bacteria
- **organism**: a living thing
- **interact**: to act on or affect each other
- **adapt**: to change or fit oneself to the conditions of the place where one lives
- **minerals**: natural substances found in soil

## Practice questions

1. What is an environment?
2. Name two abiotic factors in an environment.
3. Name two biotic factors in an environment.
4. What does it mean when organisms adapt to their environment?
5. Look at your classroom. Name one abiotic factor and one biotic factor you can see or feel there.

## Answers

1. An environment is the surroundings or space in which a person, animal or plant lives. It is everything that surrounds us.
2. Sunlight, air, water and minerals in soil are abiotic factors. Any two of these are correct.
3. Plants, animals and bacteria are biotic factors. Any two of these are correct.
4. Organisms adapt means they change or fit themselves to suit the conditions of their environment.
5. Sunlight coming through the window is an abiotic factor, and a student or a plant in the room is a biotic factor. Any correct pair is acceptable.
