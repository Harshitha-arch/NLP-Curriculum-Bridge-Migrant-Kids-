## What you already know

You know that trees have tall, hard, thick stems, like a mango tree. In this lesson, you will learn that some trees give the wood we use to build houses and make furniture.

## Lesson

Some plants give us wood. We call this wood **timber**. Timber is used to build houses and to make furniture, such as chairs, tables and desks.

Good timber is useful for several reasons. It lasts a long time, so it is strong and does not wear out quickly. It has a stylish finish, so it looks good. It can also stand up to changes in temperature, so it does not get damaged easily when the weather gets hot or cold.

Not all timber is the same. Timber is sorted into two groups, **hardwood** and **softwood**. The sorting is based on the structure of the wood, which means how the wood is built inside.

**Hardwood** comes from angiosperms. These are flowering plants, and they are the largest group of land plants. Hardwood is used for high-quality furniture, desks and flooring. It is also used for wooden construction. Teak and jackfruit are examples of hardwood trees. Think of a good-quality wooden desk in a school. That desk is made of hardwood.

**Softwood** comes from gymnosperms. These are non-flowering plants, and their trees are called softwood trees. Some flowering plants also give softwood. Softwood is used to make plywood, wooden boxes, and paper. Katampu and pine are examples of softwood trees.

So, the main difference is this. Hardwood comes from flowering plants and is used for good furniture and building. Softwood comes from non-flowering plants, and some flowering plants, and is used for boxes, plywood and paper.

## Key words

- **Timber**: wood that is obtained from plants and used for building and making things
- **Durability**: the ability to last a long time without breaking or wearing out
- **Hardwood**: timber that comes from flowering plants and is used for good furniture and building
- **Softwood**: timber that comes from non-flowering plants and some flowering plants, and is used for boxes, plywood and paper
- **Angiosperm**: a flowering plant
- **Gymnosperm**: a non-flowering plant
- **Classified**: sorted into groups

## Practice questions

1. Name two features of timber that make it useful.
2. How are hardwood and softwood different?
3. Name one hardwood tree and one softwood tree from the lesson.
4. Which kind of timber is used to make a good-quality desk?
5. Why do you think a wooden box and a good-quality wooden table might be made from different kinds of timber?

## Answers

1. Timber is useful because it is durable, has a stylish finish and can resist changes in temperature.
2. Hardwood comes from flowering plants and is used for good furniture, desks, flooring and building. Softwood comes from non-flowering plants and some flowering plants, and is used for plywood, boxes and paper.
3. Teak is a hardwood tree, and katampu is a softwood tree. Jackfruit is a hardwood example, and pine is a softwood example.
4. Good-quality furniture and desks are made only from hardwood.
5. Wooden boxes are made from softwood, and good furniture is made from hardwood, so the two uses need timber of different kinds.
