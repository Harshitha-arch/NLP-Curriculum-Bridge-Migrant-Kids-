## What you already know

Think about a mobile phone. You can touch its screen, and it also runs apps that help you read, play, or talk to people. In this lesson you will learn that a computer has two parts that work together, one you can touch and one you cannot.

## Lesson

Today we learn about two parts of a computer: **hardware** and **software**.

A computer is a device with two parts. The first part is hardware. The second part is software. Both parts work together to make the computer useful.

Hardware is the part of the computer that you can see and touch. A monitor is a hardware device. It shows the output of the computer on its screen.

Software is the part you cannot touch. It tells the computer what to do.

Now let us see how they work together. First, a hardware device helps you enter **input**. Input is the information you put into the computer. Next, the software **processes** the input data. To process means to work on the information and change it. Last, the computer gives the **output**. Output is the result, and it appears on the monitor.

Here is an easy way to remember it. The computer is like a human body. The body is the hardware. The soul is the software. The body cannot work alone, and the soul gives it direction. In the same way, a computer needs both hardware and software to function.

Remember:
- Hardware is the part you can touch.
- Software is the part you cannot touch.
- Both are needed for a computer to work.

## Key words

- **Hardware**: the parts of a computer that you can touch and see.
- **Software**: the instructions that tell the computer what to do. You cannot touch it.
- **Device**: a machine or tool made for a special job.
- **Input**: information that you put into a computer.
- **Process**: to work on information and change it.
- **Output**: the result that the computer gives you.
- **Monitor**: a hardware device that shows the output on a screen.

## Practice questions

1. What are the two parts of a computer?
2. Which part of a computer is the monitor, hardware or software?
3. What does the software do with the input data?
4. In the body example, what is the hardware and what is the software?

## Answers

1. A computer has two parts: hardware and software.
2. The monitor is hardware, because it is a device that shows the output.
3. The software processes the input data and then gives the output.
4. In the example, the body is the hardware and the soul is the software.

## Note for the teacher

This lesson is based only on the SCERT passage on Hardware and Software, and it uses the passage's body and soul comparison as the everyday example. The passage does not explain the types of software or open source software, even though these appear in its learning objectives, so they are not taught here and may need a separate lesson or extra material.
