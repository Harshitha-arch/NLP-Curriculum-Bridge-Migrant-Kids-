## What you already know

You may remember from your NCERT book that water is used for drinking, cooking, bathing and farming. In this lesson, you will learn that water can be found as ice, as a liquid, and as a gas in the air.

## Lesson

Today we learn about the three forms of water. Water can be solid, liquid or gas.

Look at the ice on the top of very tall mountains. This ice is water in a solid form. The same solid water is found in ice bergs, ice caps, glaciers and polar regions. A glacier is a huge slow-moving mass of ice. Polar regions are the very cold areas near the North and South ends of the Earth.

Now think about the sea, a lake or a river. The water there is liquid. Liquid water can flow and take the shape of its container. Liquid water is found in oceans, seas, lakes and rivers. Some liquid water is also found underground, below the surface of the land.

Water can also be a gas. We call this gas water vapour. We cannot always see it, but it is present in the air around us. Water vapour is the gaseous form of water.

So, water is found in three forms:

- Solid: ice in mountains, glaciers and polar regions.
- Liquid: water in oceans, seas, lakes, rivers and underground.
- Gas: water vapour in the air around us.

Water is very important for all living things on the Earth. We must learn to use water carefully and to save it. Many people make efforts to save water, and we should appreciate them.

## Key words

- **Solid**: a form that keeps its own shape, like ice.
- **Liquid**: a form that flows, like the water in a river.
- **Gas**: a form that spreads out and fills the air around us.
- **Vapour**: water in the gas form, which is in the air.
- **Glacier**: a very large mass of ice that moves slowly on land.
- **Polar region**: the very cold area near the North or South end of the Earth.
- **Iceberg**: a big piece of ice floating in the sea.
- **Ice cap**: a thick layer of ice that covers land at the top of a mountain or near the poles.
- **Underground**: below the surface of the land.
- **Conserve**: to use something carefully so that it is not wasted.

## Practice questions

1. Name the three forms of water.
2. Where is solid water found?
3. Name two places where liquid water is found.
4. Where is water vapour found?
5. Name one place near you where you can see liquid water. Which form of water is it?

## Answers

1. The three forms of water are solid, liquid and gas.
2. Solid water is found in ice bergs, ice caps, glaciers and polar regions, and on top of tall mountains.
3. Liquid water is found in oceans, seas, lakes and rivers. It is also found underground.
4. Water vapour is present in the air around us.
5. Answers may vary. For example, a river near my home has liquid water.
