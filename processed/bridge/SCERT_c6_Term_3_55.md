## What you already know

From your NCERT lessons, you learned that plants can be grouped as herbs, shrubs and trees by their height and stem. Hibiscus, which you may have seen on a nature walk, is a plant that people also grow in gardens to make them look beautiful.

## Lesson

Some plants are grown because they look beautiful. These plants are called **ornamental plants**. We grow them to decorate our houses, gardens and parks.

Ornamental plants are grown for **aesthetic** reasons. Aesthetic means related to beauty, or how something looks pleasing to the eye.

Many ornamental plants are grown for their flowers. Jasmine, rose, chrysanthemum, carnation and gerbera are examples. Growing flowering plants is an important part of **horticulture**, which is the work of growing plants.

Ornamental plants can be grouped by their size and how they grow, just as you learned in NCERT:

- **Shrubs**: Hibiscus, grape, jasmine and croton are shrubs. Shrubs are smaller than trees and have many branches close to the ground.
- **Climbers**: Mullai, allamanda and bougainvillea are climbers. A climber is a plant that grows up and along something to support itself.
- **Trees**: Golden shower tree and mandarai (Delonix) are trees. Mandarai is also called "flame of the forest".

Notice that jasmine is named as both a flower plant and a shrub. Some plants can belong to more than one group. Think about this: you have learned that a rose is a shrub in NCERT. Ornamental plants are not always grouped by their height alone. We also group them by why people grow them, which is for beauty.

Now think about the plants near your home, school or temple. Which of them are grown only to look beautiful? Which are shrubs, climbers or trees? Look closely, and you will see that ornamental plants make our surroundings pleasant.

## Key words

- **Ornamental plant**: a plant grown to decorate and look beautiful
- **Aesthetic**: related to beauty or how something looks pleasing
- **Horticulture**: the work of growing plants
- **Shrub**: a plant with many branches close to the ground, smaller than a tree
- **Climber**: a plant that grows up and along a support
- **Tree**: a tall plant with a single thick, woody stem

## Practice questions

1. What are ornamental plants?
2. Name two flower plants that are grown as ornamental plants.
3. Why do people grow ornamental plants in houses, gardens and parks?
4. Name one shrub and one climber from the lesson.
5. Think about a plant near your home. Is it an ornamental plant? Which group does it belong to: shrub, climber or tree?

## Answers

1. Ornamental plants are plants that are grown to decorate and look beautiful.
2. Jasmine, rose, chrysanthemum, carnation and gerbera are flower plants grown as ornamental plants. Any two of these are correct.
3. People grow ornamental plants to decorate houses, gardens and parks.
4. Hibiscus is a shrub and mullai is a climber. Any one shrub and one climber from the lesson are correct.
5. Students should name a plant they see near home and say whether it is a shrub, climber or tree. Accept any answer that is based on what the student observes.
