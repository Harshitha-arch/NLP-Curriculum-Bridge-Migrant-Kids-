## What you already know

You know that solar panels on rooftops and street lights capture the Sun's energy and make electricity. Electricity also lights our bulbs and runs our fans and clocks.

## Lesson

Electricity lights our homes, runs our fans, and makes our work easy. Where does it come from?

One source is the Sun. Solar panels catch the Sun's energy and make electricity. But there are other sources too. We get electricity from:

- **thermal power** plants
- **hydel power** plants
- **tidal power** plants
- **wind power**
- **solar power**

Power plants are not built everywhere. They are built where the **natural resources** they need are found. A natural resource is something we get from nature. For example, Neyveli in Tamil Nadu has a thermal power plant because lignite, a natural resource, is found there.

Wind mills also need the right place. They are placed where the wind is strong. Near the hills of Tirunelveli District, there are wind mills because the wind is good there.

Electricity can also come from **electric cells** and **batteries**. A cell or battery stores energy and gives it out as electricity. Your wall clock needs electrical energy to work.

Think about your grandfather's time. People used oil lamps for light. They cooked on fires of wood or coal. Electricity has changed this. Many of our daily tasks are now easy, and the world is, as the lesson says, "on our hands."

Electricity goes through **electric circuits**. Some materials let electricity flow through them. These are **conductors**. Materials that do not let electricity flow through are **insulators**. We will learn more about these in the next lessons.

## Key words

- **Thermal power**: electricity made from heat energy at a power plant.
- **Hydel power**: electricity made from the energy of flowing water.
- **Tidal power**: electricity made from the energy of the sea.
- **Wind power**: electricity made from the energy of the wind.
- **Solar power**: electricity made from the energy of the Sun.
- **Natural resources**: things we get from nature.
- **Electric cells**: small sources that give out electricity.
- **Batteries**: sources made of cells that give out electricity.
- **Appliances**: machines or devices that work on electricity.
- **Conductors**: materials that let electricity flow through them.

## Practice questions

1. Name any three sources of electricity.
2. Why is there a thermal power plant in Neyveli?
3. Why are wind mills placed near the hills of Tirunelveli District?
4. Where can electricity come from apart from power plants?
5. Name one thing in your home that works on electricity.

## Answers

1. Any three of: thermal power, hydel power, tidal power, wind power, solar power.
2. There is a thermal power plant in Neyveli because lignite, a natural resource, is available there.
3. Wind mills are placed there because the area has a good wind resource.
4. Electricity can also come from electric cells and batteries.
5. A fan, a clock, a bulb, or any appliance in your home that runs on electricity.
