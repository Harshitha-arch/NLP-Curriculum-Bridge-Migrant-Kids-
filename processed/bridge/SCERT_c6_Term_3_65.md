## What you already know

When you buy a ticket to watch a movie, you pay so that you can watch it. Some computer programs work the same way: you must pay before you can use them.

## Lesson

Some software costs money. Software is the set of instructions that tells a computer what to do. You may have used software to play a game or type a letter.

Some software is **paid and proprietary**. "Paid" means you must pay for it. "Proprietary" means the software belongs to its maker, and the maker controls how it is used.

You cannot use paid software just by installing it. You need a **license**, which is a permission to use the software. The maker gives you a license only after you **purchase**, or buy, the software. No purchase, no license.

Paid software can be used for a long time (permanently) or for a short time (temporarily). Either way, you need a license.

People who use software are called **end users**. End users must not steal paid software. They must also not use a **pirated** copy. A pirated copy is an illegal copy made without permission. The law forbids both.

Here are some examples of paid and proprietary software:

- Windows
- Microsoft Office
- Adobe Photoshop

Think about a friend who gives you a copy of Adobe Photoshop on a pen drive. If the copy was not bought and given with a license, it may be a pirated copy. Using it is not allowed.

## Key words

- **Software**: instructions that tell a computer what to do.
- **Paid**: you must pay money to use it.
- **Proprietary**: owned and controlled by its maker.
- **License**: a permission to use software.
- **Purchase**: to buy.
- **Permanently**: for a long time, without an end date.
- **Temporarily**: for a short time only.
- **End user**: the person who uses the software.
- **Pirated**: an illegal copy made without permission.
- **Legally**: according to the law.

## Practice questions

1. What is a license?
2. Why can a user not get a license for paid software unless they buy it?
3. Name two examples of paid and proprietary software.
4. Your friend offers you a free copy of Adobe Photoshop that was not bought. What should you do, and why?

## Answers

1. A license is a permission to use software.
2. The maker gives the license only when the software is purchased.
3. Windows and Microsoft Office are examples. (Adobe Photoshop is also correct.)
4. Do not use it, because a copy that was not bought is a pirated copy, and using pirated software is illegal.
