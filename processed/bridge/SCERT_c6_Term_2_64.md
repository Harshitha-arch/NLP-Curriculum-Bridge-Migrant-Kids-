## What you already know
Your body does many jobs without you asking, such as feeling hungry before lunch or sleepy at night. Different parts of the body must work together to keep these jobs going.

## Lesson
Your body has many parts that work together. One group of body parts is called the **endocrine system**.

The endocrine system has **endocrine glands**. A gland is a body part that makes something. Endocrine glands are present in your body. They produce chemical substances called **hormones**. A chemical substance is a kind of material.

The endocrine system does two main things. First, it **regulates** many **functions** of the body. Regulate means to control. A function is a job. So the endocrine system controls many of the jobs your body does. Second, it **maintains the internal environment**. The internal environment means the conditions inside your body. Maintain means to keep something in good order.

Your sense organs are also important. Your eyes, ears and skin need care.

- **Eyes:** Do not read in very bright light or very dim light. Do not read in a moving vehicle. Do not look at TV, computer, laptop or cell phone screens for a long time. Do not rub your eyes harshly. Wash your eyes gently with clean water two or three times a day.
- **Ears:** Protect your ears from hard blows. Never prick your ears with toothpicks or hairpins. This is dangerous. It may puncture the eardrum, which means make a hole in it, and cause an ear infection.
- **Skin:** Bathe at least once a day to keep your skin clean and fresh.

## Key words
- **Endocrine system**: a group of body parts that controls many jobs of the body
- **Endocrine glands**: body parts that make hormones
- **Hormones**: chemical substances made by endocrine glands
- **Regulates**: controls
- **Functions**: jobs done by the body
- **Internal environment**: the conditions inside the body
- **Maintains**: keeps something in good order
- **Sense organs**: body parts that help us sense the world, such as eyes, ears and skin
- **Puncture**: make a small hole in something
- **Infection**: an illness caused by germs getting into the body

## Practice questions
1. What do endocrine glands produce?
2. Name the two main jobs of the endocrine system.
3. Why is it dangerous to prick your ears with a toothpick?
4. Name two things you should not do to take care of your eyes.
5. Your friend rubs her eyes hard after reading. What should she do instead?

## Answers
1. Endocrine glands produce hormones, which are chemical substances.
2. The endocrine system regulates many functions of the body and maintains the internal environment.
3. It may puncture the eardrum and cause an ear infection.
4. You should not read in very bright or very dim light, and you should not rub your eyes harshly. (Any one of the eye-care rules in the lesson is also correct.)
5. She should wash her eyes gently with clean water instead.
