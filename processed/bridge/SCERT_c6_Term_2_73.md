## What you already know

You may have seen a charger or earphones with a wire that plugs into something. Some things do not need a wire at all, and a computer can work the same way.

## Lesson

Today we learn about **wireless connections**. "Wireless" means there are no wires or cables between the devices.

Many devices must be connected to a computer. Usually, we connect them with a cable. But there are two ways to connect without cables. These are **Bluetooth** and **Wi-Fi**.

**Bluetooth** is a wireless connection. It lets a device connect to a computer without a cable. For example, a **mouse** and a **keyboard** can be connected to the computer using Bluetooth. Bluetooth also lets data be shared with devices that are **nearby**. "Nearby" means close by.

**Wi-Fi** is another wireless connection. Wi-Fi helps us connect to the **internet** without any connecting cables. The internet is a large network that lets computers share information. With Wi-Fi, data can be shared from anywhere.

So, Bluetooth and Wi-Fi are both wireless connections. Both help devices connect without cables. Bluetooth is for nearby devices, such as a mouse or keyboard. Wi-Fi is for connecting to the internet and sharing data from anywhere.

Let us remember:
- Wireless means no cables.
- Bluetooth connects a mouse or keyboard to a computer and shares data with nearby devices.
- Wi-Fi connects to the internet without cables and shares data from anywhere.

## Key words

- **Wireless**: connected without wires or cables.
- **Bluetooth**: a wireless connection that links devices that are close to each other.
- **Wi-Fi**: a wireless connection that links a device to the internet.
- **Internet**: a large network that lets computers share information.
- **Cable**: a wire that joins two devices.
- **Device**: a machine or tool, such as a mouse, keyboard or computer.
- **Data**: information that can be stored, sent or shared.
- **Connect**: to join one thing to another.
- **Nearby**: close by.

## Practice questions

1. What does "wireless" mean?
2. Name two wireless connections from the lesson.
3. Name one device that can be connected to a computer using Bluetooth.
4. What can Bluetooth do with nearby devices?
5. Why might a wireless mouse be easier to use than a mouse with a cable?

## Answers

1. "Wireless" means connected without wires or cables.
2. Bluetooth and Wi-Fi are the two wireless connections.
3. A mouse or a keyboard can be connected to a computer using Bluetooth.
4. Bluetooth lets data be shared with nearby devices.
5. A wireless mouse connects without a cable, so there is no cable to get in the way.
