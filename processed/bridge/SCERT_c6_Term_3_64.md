## What you already know

You may have used apps on a phone or a computer, such as a game, a music player, or a drawing app. Some of these cost money to use, and some are free. Free and open source software is one kind of software that you can use without paying.

## Lesson

Today we learn about free and open source software.

**Software** is the set of programs used on a computer. LINUX, Open Office and Geogebra are examples of software.

**Free software** is available free of cost. This means you do not pay money to get it or use it.

Free and open source software can also be **shared with many end users**. An end user is a person who uses the software. So one free program can be given to many people.

Free software is also **editable**. This means the user can change it. It is also **customizable**. This means the user can adjust it to suit their own needs.

Because users can edit and customize it, free software can be updated. New software can also be developed from it.

Here are some examples of free and open source software:

- LINUX
- Open Office
- Geogebra

Open Office is free and open source software. You can use it without paying, and you can share it with others.

Remember these points:

1. Free software is available at no cost.
2. It can be shared with many end users.
3. It can be edited and customized by the user.
4. LINUX, Open Office and Geogebra are examples.

## Key words

- **Free of cost**: you do not have to pay money for it.
- **End user**: a person who uses the software.
- **Editable**: can be changed by the user.
- **Customizable**: can be adjusted to suit the user's needs.
- **Software**: a set of programs used on a computer.
- **Open source software**: free and open software that users can share and change.

## Practice questions

1. What does "free of cost" mean?
2. Name two examples of free and open source software.
3. Why can free software be called editable?
4. What can happen to free software because users can edit and customize it?
5. Your friend has a free program on a computer. Can you also use it, and why?

## Answers

1. "Free of cost" means you do not have to pay money for the software.
2. LINUX, Open Office and Geogebra are examples of free and open source software. Any two of these are correct.
3. Free software is editable because the user can change it.
4. Because users can edit and customize it, the software can be updated, and new software can be developed.
5. Yes, because free and open source software can be shared with many end users.
