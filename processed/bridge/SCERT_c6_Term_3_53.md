## What you already know
You may have seen tulsi, aloe or neem growing near a house or a temple. Your family may have used a plant leaf or a home remedy when you had a cut or a cold.

## Lesson
Some plants around us can help to heal our diseases. We call these plants **medicinal plants**. A disease is an illness that makes our body unwell.

Medicinal plants can help with many problems. They can ease burns, cuts, colds, fever and sneezes. They can also help with other health problems.

Why do these plants help? Some plants have **chemical compounds**. A compound is a substance made of two or more parts joined together. Some of these compounds act against insects, **fungi** and certain **germs**. Fungi are tiny living things, and germs are tiny living things that can cause disease.

Medicinal plants are rich sources of **ingredients**. An ingredient is one of the parts that is mixed together to make something. These ingredients can be used to make medicine.

Let us look at some plants with high medicinal value.

**Amla:** We use its fruit. It cures diseases caused by a lack of vitamin C, such as scurvy. It also helps to improve **immunity**. Immunity is the body's ability to fight illness.

**Tulsi:** We use its leaves and seeds. It is used for cough, cold and bronchitis. It also acts as an **expectorant**. An expectorant helps to bring out the mucus from the chest.

**Aloe:** We use its leaves. It is used as a laxative, for wound healing, and for skin burns and ulcers. A laxative helps the body to pass stool.

**Neem:** We use its bark, leaves and seeds. It is used for skin diseases. It is also a germicide, which means it kills germs.

**Turmeric:** We use its **rhizome**, which is the part of the plant used as medicine. It helps the body to fight foreign invaders, which are harmful things that enter the body.

Remember: medicinal plants are useful, but we should use them with care and proper guidance.

## Key words
- **Medicinal plant**: a plant that can be used to heal diseases.
- **Healing**: making a hurt or illness better.
- **Alleviate**: to make pain or illness less strong.
- **Compound**: a substance made of two or more parts joined together.
- **Fungi**: tiny living things that can cause disease.
- **Germ**: a tiny living thing that can cause disease.
- **Ingredient**: one of the parts mixed together to make something, such as medicine.
- **Immunity**: the body's ability to fight illness.
- **Deficiency**: a shortage of something the body needs, such as vitamin C.
- **Expectorant**: something that helps bring mucus out of the chest.

## Practice questions
1. What do we call plants that can heal diseases?
2. Which part of the amla plant is used as medicine?
3. Why do some medicinal plants act against germs?
4. Why is turmeric useful for the body?
5. Your friend has a cough. Which plant from the lesson is used for cough, and which part of it is used?

## Answers
1. We call these plants medicinal plants.
2. The fruit of the amla plant is used as medicine.
3. Some plants have chemical compounds that act against insects, fungi and certain germs.
4. Turmeric helps the body to fight foreign invaders.
5. Tulsi is used for cough. Its leaves and seeds are used.
