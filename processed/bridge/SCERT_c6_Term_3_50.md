## What you already know
From NCERT, you know that wheat, rice and maize give us carbohydrates, which give us energy, and that pulses are good sources of protein. In this lesson, you will learn which parts of plants give us these foods.

## Lesson
Plants are the main source of food for human beings. Plants that we eat are called **food plants**.

Think about a vegetable garden at home, or vegetables sold in the market. Have you seen **harvesting**? Harvesting means picking a crop when it is ready.

We do not eat only one part of a plant. We eat roots, stems, leaves, seeds, unripe fruits and ripe fruits. Vegetables come from different parts of plants:

- **Roots:** beetroot, carrot
- **Leaves:** curry leaves, cabbage, green vegetables
- **Stems:** potato, yam, sugarcane
- **Flowers:** banana flower, cauliflower
- **Fruits:** amla, guava

Some plant foods come from grass. **Cereals** are the edible grains of **cultivated** grass. Cultivated means grown by people on farms. Rice, wheat, bajra, millet and ragi are cereals.

Since ancient times, rice, wheat and millet have been the **staple food** of a very large population of India. A staple food is the main food people eat every day. Old grains that were burnt and found at digging sites show that people ate them long ago.

**Pulses** are the edible seeds of plants in the legume family. Pulses are produced in **pods**, which are the long covers that hold seeds. Bengal gram and green mung bean are examples.

Plants give us more than food. They give us coffee, tea, sugar and raw materials for oil. **Economic botany** is the study of the relationship between people and plants, and the uses of plants in the economy. Plants help people with food, clothing, shelter and medicine. Plants also bring money to the country. A country's wealth depends a lot on its farming and plant products.

## Key words
- **food plants**: plants that we eat as food
- **harvesting**: picking a crop when it is ready
- **cereals**: edible grains of cultivated grass, such as rice and wheat
- **pulses**: edible seeds of plants in the legume family, grown in pods
- **pods**: long covers that hold seeds
- **staple food**: the main food that people eat every day
- **cultivated**: grown by people on farms
- **edible**: safe to eat
- **economic botany**: the study of how people and plants are related, and how plants are used in the economy
- **economy**: the money and business of a country

## Practice questions
1. Name any three parts of a plant that we eat as food.
2. Give one example of a vegetable that comes from a stem.
3. What are cereals?
4. Where are pulses produced?
5. A meal has rice, cabbage and green mung bean. Which of these is a cereal, which is a vegetable, and which is a pulse?

## Answers
1. We eat roots, stems, leaves, seeds, unripe fruits and ripe fruits. Any three of these are correct.
2. Potato, yam and sugarcane come from stems.
3. Cereals are the edible grains of cultivated grass, such as rice, wheat, bajra, millet and ragi.
4. Pulses are produced in pods.
5. Rice is a cereal, cabbage is a vegetable, and green mung bean is a pulse.
