## What you already know

You learned that every magnet has two poles, a North pole and a South pole. You also learned that unlike poles attract each other and like poles push away from each other.

## Lesson

Magnets are useful, but they can lose their magnetic power if we do not store them properly. This is called **improper storage**. So we need to know how to keep magnets safe.

**Bar magnets** are long, straight magnets. To store bar magnets safely:

- Keep them in **pairs**. Two bar magnets go together.
- Place them with their **unlike poles** on the same side. Unlike poles are a North pole and a South pole. So one magnet's North pole sits on the same side as the other magnet's South pole.
- Put a **piece of wood** between the two magnets. This keeps them apart.
- Place **two pieces of soft iron** across their ends. Soft iron is a kind of iron that is soft. These pieces of iron help keep the magnets safe.

**Horse-shoe magnets** are curved, like a U. They need only one piece of soft iron. This piece is called a **keeper**. Place the keeper across the two poles of the horse-shoe magnet.

Remember these steps. Pairs, unlike poles on the same side, wood in between, and soft iron at the ends keep magnets safe.

## Key words

- **improper storage**: keeping something in a way that is not correct
- **bar magnet**: a long, straight magnet
- **horse-shoe magnet**: a magnet bent in the shape of a U
- **pole**: an end of a magnet, either North or South
- **unlike poles**: a North pole and a South pole
- **soft iron**: a piece of iron that is soft
- **keeper**: a piece of soft iron placed across the poles of a magnet to keep it safe

## Practice questions

1. How should bar magnets be kept for safe storage?
2. What must be placed between the two bar magnets?
3. What must be placed across the ends of the bar magnets?
4. What is a keeper, and for which magnet is a single keeper used?
5. Why should we store magnets carefully at home or in school?

## Answers

1. Bar magnets should be kept in pairs, with their unlike poles on the same side.
2. A piece of wood must be placed between the two bar magnets.
3. Two pieces of soft iron should be placed across the ends of the bar magnets.
4. A keeper is a piece of soft iron placed across the poles of a magnet. A single keeper is used for a horse-shoe magnet.
5. Improper storage can make magnets lose their properties, so we store them carefully to keep them safe.
