Activity 4: Preparation of Vermi compost
Dig a pit for about one feet depth in the 
backyard or garden of your home or school. 
Fill the pit by bio wastes, paper and food 
wastes and   place few earth worms in it, 
sprinkle water and close the place with 
jute or cardboard and ensure moisture all 
the time.
After 45 days the vermi compost casting 
layer formed just above the pit. These 
compost can be applied to the plant, which contains water soluble nutrients. This type 
of compost helps in plant growth as well as sustain the land is fertility.
