2.5 Water cycle
The water on the 
earth evaporates into the 
atmosphere due to the 
heat of the sun. The water 
vapour in the atmosphere forms clouds. 
From the clouds water falls on the earth in 
the form of rain or snow. By this natural 
process, water gets renewed. This is called 
water cycle.
Water cycle is a continuous process. 
It involves three stages - evaporation, 
condensation and precipitation.  It is 
also called the hydrological cycle.
