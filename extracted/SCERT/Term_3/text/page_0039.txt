Activity 2:  Preparation of Soap
Materials Required: 35 ml of water
10 g. of Lye (Sodium hydroxide) 60 ml 
of coconut oil.
Process : Cover your work area with 
old newspaper. Take 35 ml of water 
in a jar. Add 10 gram of concentrated 
sodium hydroxide and allow it to cool. 
Then add 60 ml of coconut oil drop by 
drop and stir it well. Pour that solution 
into an empty match box, soap can be 
obtained after getting dried. 
Try this soap to wash your handkerchief.
