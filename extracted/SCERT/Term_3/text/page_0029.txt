be able to know what happens 
to water when it is taken out 
of nature, into our house and 
once it leaves our houses.

ICT Corner
Step 1: Type the URL or scan the QR code to launch the activity.
Step 2 : 
A page of 3 games will open, click on the first game 'Melbourne water cycle', 
and click the "Play the Melbourne water game" button to start the game.
Step 3: 
Play the game by following the instructions and using the navigation keys. 
Observe the steps of water usage and the process of recycling the used 
water.
Step 4: 
Play the other two games to know about the Natural water cycle and Sources 
of water.
