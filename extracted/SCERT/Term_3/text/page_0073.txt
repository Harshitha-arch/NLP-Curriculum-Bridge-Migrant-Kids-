Cereals
Cereals are edible components of grain 
of cultivated grass.  Example Rice, Wheat, 
Bajra, Millet and Ragi.
Pulses
Pulses are edible seeds of plants in 
the legume family. Pulses are produced in 
pods. eg: Bengal gram, Green mung bean.
