1.9 Storage of Magnets
Improper storage can also cause magnets to lose 
their properties. To keep them safe, bar magnets 
should be kept in pairs with their unlike poles on the 
same side. They must be separated by a piece of 
wood and two pieces of soft iron should be placed 
across their ends.
For a horse-shoe magnet a single piece of soft iron can be used as a magnetic keeper 
across the poles.
