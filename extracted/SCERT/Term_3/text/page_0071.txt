Introduction
We are living in a green planet. Plenty 
of natural plant resources are around us. 
Economic botany basically deals with all 
pervading plants in relation to human 
welfare as food, clothing, shelter and 
medicine either directly or indirectly. 
Indirect usage includes the needs 
of man’s livestock and the maintenance 
of the environment; the benefits may be 
domestic, commercial, environmental, or 
aesthetic.
Plants bring about economy to the 
country in large extent and it is a fact that 
the wealth of any country largely depends 
upon its agriculture and plant products.
Economic botany is the study 
of 
relationship 
between 
people 
and plants and the uses of plants in 
economy. 
From the earliest time rice, wheat and 
millet have been the staple food of a vast 
population of India as indicated by the 
presence of charred grains in most of the 
excavation sites. In addition, references 
are abundant in ancient literature about 
the existence and usage of several crops of 
economic importance.
