II.	Terrestrial habitat
Terrestrial habitats are the ones that 
are found on land like forest, grassland 
and desert. It also includes man-made 
habitats like farms, towns and cities. 
They can be as big as a continent or as 
small as an island. They make up about 
28% of the entire world habitat.
Example: Evergreen forest, scrub jungles.
Terrestrial habitat is classified into 
three types. They are:
a. Forest    b. Grass land    c. Desert 
a. Fresh water Habitat
Rivers, lakes, ponds and pools are the 
fresh water habitats. Water hyacinth, water 
lily and lotus are seen in the fresh water 
habitat. In these plants roots are very much 
reduced in size. Stem and leaves have air 
chambers that allow aquatic plants to float 
in water.
Air spaces in stems
