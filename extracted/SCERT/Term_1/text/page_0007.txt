1. 1 Length
What is length? The distance between 
one point and the other desired point is 
known as length. It may be the distance 
between the edges of your book or the 
corners of the football ground in your 
school or even from your home to school.
The standard unit of length is 
'metre'. It is represented by the letter 
‘m’. Very small lengths can be measured 
in millimetre (mm) and centimetre (cm). 
Larger measures, say height of a building, 
length of a banner or height of a lamp post 
are all measured in metre. How to express 
still longer lengths say, distance between 
two cities or villages or distance between 
your school and home? It is expressed in 
kilometre (km).
Know the unit of length
1 km (kilometre)	 =	1000 m (metre)
1 m   (metre)	
=	100 cm (centimetre)
1 cm (centimetre)	=	10 mm (millimetre)
