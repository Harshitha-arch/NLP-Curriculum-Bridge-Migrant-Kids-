Fact file
Thar Desert, also called Great Indian 
Desert, is an arid region of rolling 
sand hills on the Indian subcontinent. 
It is located partly in Rajasthan state, 
north-western India, and partly in 
Punjab and Sindh (Sind) provinces, 
Eastern Pakistan.
