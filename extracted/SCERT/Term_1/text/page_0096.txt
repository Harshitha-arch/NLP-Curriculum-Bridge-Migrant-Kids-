Bacteria 
Bacteria are very small prokaryotic 
microorganisms. Bacterial cells do not 
have nucleus and do not usually have 
membrane bound organelles.
•	 Bacteria can exist either as independent 
organisms or as parasites.
•	 They invade tissues.
•	 They produce pus or harmful wastes.

6.3 Introduction to Microbes 
When you neglect personal hygiene, 
you are increasing the risk of falling sick.
Let us name some of the diseases or 
conditions caused by microorganism due 
to the negligence of personal hygiene.
1.  Diarrhoea	
  2.  Tooth decay
3.  Athlete’s foot (Madurai’s foot)
4.  Dandruff.
Do you believe that there are some 
organisms which you cannot see with 
your naked eye? Yes. microbes can not 
be seen without the help of a microscope.
Most of the microbes belong to four 
major groups.
•	 Bacteria
•	 Virus
•	 Protozoa
•	 fungi
