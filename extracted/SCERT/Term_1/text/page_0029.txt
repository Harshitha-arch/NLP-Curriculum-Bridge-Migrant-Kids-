Observe the formula for speed. If 
we denote the distance in metre and 
time by second then the unit of speed 
is metre/second. If we denote the 
distance in kilometre and time in hour 
then the unit of speed is kilometre/
hour. Sometimes we use units like 
centimetre/second.
In science we generally use SI units. 
In SI units the unit of distance is metre 
and the unit of time is second. So, the SI 
unit of speed is metre/second.
