Points to Remember
 The skeletal system gives shape to 
the body and protects the soft internal 
organs.
 There are three types of muscles – 
skeletal muscle (voluntary), smooth 
muscle (involuntary)  and cardiac 
muscle.
 Circulatory system constitutes the 
heart,  blood vessels and blood.
 Diaphragm – A large flat muscle 
forming the floor at the chest cavity.
